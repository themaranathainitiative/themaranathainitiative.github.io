Maranatha Initiative is a place for my thoughts and reflections on Scripture, theology, current events, culture, and whatever else seems worth writing about.

I'm Benjamin Perkins, a young man trying to understand the Bible faithfully and make sense of the world around me.

Some of what I write will be carefully developed arguments. Other posts may be questions, observations, or ideas I'm still working through. I don't claim to have everything figured out, and I expect my understanding to grow and change.

My hope is simply to think carefully, write honestly, and encourage others to do the same.
