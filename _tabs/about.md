I'm Benjamin Perkins, a young man trying to understand the Bible faithfully and make sense of the world around me. 

Whether you've stumbled upon this website or willingly found it, I welcome you. Maranatha Initiative is my personal collection of reflections on theology, current events, culture, and whatever else seems worth writing about. Some of what I write will be carefully developed arguments. Other posts may be questions, observations, or ideas I'm still working through. I am only a man learning to reason from the Scriptures. Perhaps I have some insight you may glean from my ramblings. My desire is simple: to think carefully, write honestly, and encourage others to do the same. 

May God bless you today.

*Statement on AI*
I will never use AI to generate my writings. I occaisionally use generative AI to suggest *minor gramatical corrections*, but AI has no place in the development of my thoughts/reflections. Any instance of the em-dash (—) is because I intentionally put it there.
